SimForge

It hands the same city and the same trips to three traffic simulators, then compares what comes out. One step took 142 hours. I got it down to 7 by caching routes and running on 16 supercomputer cores. 668 automated tests and a container rebuilt on every release keep every run repeatable.

Python, Docker, GitHub Actions, SLURM, Ohio Supercomputer Center. Paper under review at IEEE T-ITS.

Cityscape

An open-source C++ model that builds a synthetic city from census and map data. It was putting 70% of homes on buildings that didn’t exist. I traced that to one misconfigured filter. Three merged pull requests later, 500,000+ buildings were back and 94% of homes sit on real ones.

C++, OpenMP, OpenStreetMap, US Census data. First-author paper, revision submitted to Frontiers.
